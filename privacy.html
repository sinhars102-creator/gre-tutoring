Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This site is run by Rahul Sinha, an individual GRE tutor based in Kolkata, India ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the site collects, why, and what you can ask me to do with it. If you have any question about it, email rahul@myresumeiq.io.

What I collect

When you send the request form, I receive:

  • your name, email address and WhatsApp number;
  • what you want help with (Quant, Verbal or both), your planned test date and your latest Verbal and Quant scores;
  • anything you choose to write in the message box;
  • the region the site detected for you (India, US or Europe), used only to pre-fill the phone country code.

I don't ask for payment details on this site. If you go on to buy sessions, payment happens separately by UPI or bank transfer.

How I use it

  • To reply to your request and arrange your free mock tests and review call.
  • To plan and run your sessions if you decide to continue.

I don't sell your information, and I don't add you to mailing lists or share your details with other tutors or companies.

Where it's stored

Form responses are saved to a private Google Sheet in my Google account, through a Google Apps Script form handler. Only I have access to it.

Analytics and advertising tools

The site uses a few third-party tools to understand how visitors use it and whether my ads work. They may set cookies or similar identifiers in your browser:

  • Vercel Web Analytics: counts visits, referring sites, countries and device types.
  • Microsoft Clarity: records anonymised sessions and heatmaps of how pages are used. Form inputs are masked, so what you type isn't captured.
  • Google Ads tag: tells Google Ads when someone who clicked an ad sends the form, so I can measure ad performance.
  • Reddit Pixel and Reddit Conversions API: tell Reddit Ads when someone who clicked a Reddit ad sends the form. Your email is converted to an irreversible hash before it's sent to Reddit.

Each of these providers handles data under its own privacy policy. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings, and most ad-blockers stop these tools from loading.

How long I keep it

I keep your request for as long as I'm talking to you or tutoring you, and for up to 12 months after our last contact, unless you ask me to delete it sooner.

Your choices

You can ask me at any time to see, correct or delete the information you sent. Email rahul@myresumeiq.io and I'll act on it within 30 days.

Children

This service is meant for people aged 18 and over preparing for graduate admissions. I don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update the date at the top of this page.
