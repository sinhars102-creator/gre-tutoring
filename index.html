The method

What we work on

Strategies that fix the reason you get questions wrong, not just the topic.

Both01

Keep an error ledger

Every wrong answer gets logged with the trap that caught you, not just the topic. After two weeks, the same 4–5 traps account for most of your lost points. Those are what we fix.

Verbal02

Read for structure, not vocabulary

In Text Completion and Sentence Equivalence, one signal word usually decides the answer. Find it first, predict the blank in your own words, then look at the options.

Signals to circle: but although yet notwithstanding (contrast) · and indeed ; (continuation)
Verbal03

Test SE pairs properly

Two words that mean the same thing aren't enough. Both must produce the same meaning in that sentence. Most SE mistakes come from picking a synonym pair that doesn't fit the blank.

Verbal04

Answer RC from the author's purpose

Before the questions, write one line on why the author wrote the passage. Wrong answers are usually true details that miss the point.

Quant05

Set up before you solve

On hard questions, write what's given and what's asked before calculating anything. Freezing usually happens because people try to solve in their head.

Quant06

Prove "cannot be determined"

In Quantitative Comparison, don't pick D on a hunch. Find two cases that give different results. If you can't, the answer isn't D.

Check 0, negatives, fractions, and the boundary of every < vs ≤.
Vocab07

Learn words through roots

Lists of 1,000 words don't stick. Learning roots and word families means you can guess words you've never seen.
